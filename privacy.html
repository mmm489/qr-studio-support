Privacy Policy

QR Studio works locally without an account. Cloud features, product analytics, advertising, and Pro access are clearly described and remain separate from the essential scanning tools.

Effective September 17, 2026

1. Who we are

QR Studio: Scan & Create (the “App”) is provided by Profitcentral SLU (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how the App and its optional cloud service handle information.

2. Information we collect

If you only use local features, we do not receive your scans, generated codes, photos, history, favorites, or app preferences. If you enable a cloud feature, we create a pseudonymous account identifier and process the data needed to provide that feature.

  • Cloud sync stores the QR or barcode content you choose to sync, titles, colors, favorites, and timestamps.
  • Dynamic QR codes store the redirect destination and aggregated scan details such as country or region, referrer host, browser family, and time. We do not store a full IP address for these events.
  • Apple transaction and subscription status may be processed to verify Pro access and prevent fraud. We do not receive payment card details.
  • Product interaction events are stored only if you turn on product analytics in Settings.

We do not sell personal information or use QR contents, scans, cloud content, or product analytics for advertising.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Scan and creation history is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device storage.
  • Favorites, QR colors, and related code details remain on your device.
  • Settings such as haptic feedback are stored locally in device preferences.
  • You can export your history or delete it from the App at any time.

Deleting the App normally removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

4. Optional account and cloud service

Cloud features begin with a pseudonymous account. You may optionally link it with Sign in with Apple. Access controls and row-level security restrict each account to its own content. Our cloud infrastructure provider is Supabase, hosted for QR Studio in the European Union.

You can disable future synchronization without affecting local tools. You can also delete the cloud account from Settings; this deletes the account and associated cloud data, while local records remain on your device until you delete them separately.

5. Camera and photo access

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a code. When you choose an image from Photos, the selected image is analyzed on your device and is not uploaded to us or retained by the App. Apple’s system photo picker may allow you to share only the image you select.

6. Advertising and consent

The free version may display ads supplied by Google AdMob below QR Studio’s in-app destination browser and, occasionally, before opening a scanned destination. The banner remains outside the third-party website and is not injected into its content. Pro subscribers do not see these ads. We request non-personalized ads and do not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission.

AdMob may process an IP address to estimate general location, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interactions, crash logs, and performance information to deliver, limit, report, secure, and improve ads. Non-personalized ads are not selected from your past activity, although Google may still use local storage or device identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated reporting. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your privacy choices before ads are requested. You can review or change available advertising privacy choices from Settings in the App. Refusing consent does not disable the App’s core local features; depending on local rules and technical availability, limited ads may be shown.

7. Sharing and exports

QR Studio does not share your QR content automatically. If you export a QR image or CSV history, you choose the destination through Apple’s share sheet. That destination’s privacy practices then apply. Advertising data is processed by Google only as described above.

8. External links

The App can identify and open links encoded in a QR code. QR Studio displays the destination and performs local safety checks, but websites and third-party services are controlled by their respective operators. Review the destination before opening it. QR Studio does not inject advertising into external websites.

9. Retention and children’s privacy

Cloud content is retained while the account is active or as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Transaction and advertising records may be retained by the relevant service provider where legally or operationally required. QR Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security and international processing

We use access controls, encrypted transport, restricted backend secrets, and data minimization. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Cloud data is primarily processed in the European Union; Google, Apple, or other service providers may process information in other countries under their applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App’s features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will always be published here, and material changes will be reflected in App Store privacy information.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Profitcentral SLU at contact@profitcentral.net.
