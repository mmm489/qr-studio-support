Privacy Policy

QR Studio works locally without an account. Cloud sync, dynamic QR codes, product analytics, and any future Pro features are optional and clearly described.

Effective September 6, 2026

1. Who we are

QR Studio: Scan & Create (the “App”) is provided by Profitcentral SLU (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how the App and its optional cloud service handle information.

2. Information we collect

If you only use local features, we do not receive your scans, generated codes, photos, history, favorites, or app preferences. If you enable a cloud feature, we create a pseudonymous account identifier and process the data needed to provide that feature.

  • Cloud sync stores the QR or barcode content you choose to sync, titles, colors, favorites, and timestamps.
  • Dynamic QR codes store the redirect destination and aggregated scan details such as country or region, referrer host, browser family, and time. We do not store a full IP address for these events.
  • If a future version offers paid Pro access, Apple transaction and subscription status may be processed to verify access and prevent fraud. We do not receive payment card details.
  • Product interaction events are stored only if you turn on product analytics in Settings.

We do not use this information for cross-app tracking, third-party advertising, or data brokerage.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Scan and creation history is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device storage.
  • Favorites, QR colors, and related code details remain on your device.
  • Settings such as haptic feedback are stored locally in device preferences.
  • You can export your history or delete it from the App at any time.

Deleting the App normally removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

4. Optional account and cloud service

Cloud features begin with a pseudonymous account. You may optionally link it with Sign in with Apple. Access controls and row-level security restrict each account to its own content. Our cloud infrastructure provider is Supabase, hosted for QR Studio in the European Union.

You can disable future synchronization without affecting local tools. You can also delete the cloud account from Settings; this deletes the account and associated cloud data, while local records remain on your device until you delete them separately.

5. Camera and photo access

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a code. When you choose an image from Photos, the selected image is analyzed on your device and is not uploaded to us or retained by the App. Apple’s system photo picker may allow you to share only the image you select.

6. Sharing and exports

QR Studio does not share your information automatically. If you export a QR image or CSV history, you choose the destination through Apple’s share sheet. That destination’s privacy practices then apply.

7. External links

The App can identify and open links encoded in a QR code. QR Studio displays the destination and performs local safety checks, but websites and third-party services are controlled by their respective operators. Review the destination before opening it.

8. Retention and children’s privacy

Cloud content is retained while the account is active or as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Transaction records may be retained where legally or operationally required. QR Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security and international processing

We use access controls, encrypted transport, restricted backend secrets, and data minimization. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Cloud data is primarily processed in the European Union; service providers or Apple may process information in other countries under their applicable safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App’s features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will always be published here, and material changes will be reflected in App Store privacy information.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Profitcentral SLU at contact@profitcentral.net.
