QR Studio Support

Quick answers for scanning, creating, saving, exporting, and optional cloud features.

Updated September 6, 2026

Contact support

Email contact@profitcentral.net with a short description of the issue, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or private QR content.

The camera does not open

Open iPhone Settings, find QR Studio, and allow Camera access. You can still paste a code manually or read a code from an image when camera scanning is unavailable.

Read a code from a photo

In the Scanner tab, choose Photos and select an image containing a supported QR or barcode. The image is analyzed locally and is not uploaded to us.

A link shows a warning

QR Studio checks links locally before opening them. It warns about unencrypted HTTP links, numeric IP addresses, encoded domains, embedded credentials, and unsupported schemes. Only continue if you recognize and trust the destination.

Export or delete history

Use Settings to export history as CSV or delete all saved history. Individual records can be managed from History. Deleted local records cannot be restored by QR Studio.

Cloud sync and account deletion

Cloud sync is optional and can be enabled in Settings. Local scanning, creation, history, and export continue to work without it. To permanently remove the cloud account and its associated data, choose “Delete cloud account and data” in Settings. This does not automatically delete records stored locally on your iPhone.

Purchases

The current release does not require a purchase or subscription. If a future version offers QR Studio Pro, its price and billing period will be shown by Apple before purchase and it can be managed from your Apple account.

Supported formats

QR Studio supports QR, Aztec, Data Matrix, EAN-13, EAN-8, PDF417, UPC-E, and Code 128. It can create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, events, email, phone calls, and SMS.

App details

Current release: QR Studio 1.0 for iPhone, requiring iOS 17 or later.
